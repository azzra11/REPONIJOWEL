The Adventures of Huckleberry Finn

Tells the story of a young boy, Huck Finn, who escapes his abusive father and travels
down the Mississippi River on a raft with a runaway slave named Jim, encountering
various characters and situations as they navigate the complexities
of pre-Civil War America, ultimately leading Huck to question societal norms
and develop a deep friendship with Jim despite the constraints of slavery.

Adventures of Huckleberry Finn is a picaresque novel by American author Mark Twain that
was first published in the United Kingdom in December 1884 and in the
United States in February 1885.

Originally published: December 10, 1884

Author: Mark Twain

Genres: Novel, Satire, Humor, Adventure fiction, Children's literature, Bildungsroman, Picaresque novel, Robinsonade

Characters: Huckleberry Finn, Jim, Tom Sawyer, Pap Finn, Aunt Polly, Toad of Toad Hall

Illustrator: E. W. Kemble

Pages: 362
